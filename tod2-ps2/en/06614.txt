[06614.289]
Ah, I should explain Slots first, shouldn't I.

[06614.290]
A Slot is something like an added ability that can be attached to a piece of equipment.

[06614.291]
I will explain Slots in detail separately.

[06614.292]
The simplest way to refine equipment is to refine two pieces that have one Slot each.

[06614.293]
Do that and, wonderfully, you get a piece of equipment with two Slots.

[06614.294]
One thing to watch: you can only refine equipment of the same kind, sword with sword, armor with armor.

[06614.295]
There are all sorts of other combinations, but those are for you to discover.

[06614.296]
Let me explain Slots in detail.

[06614.297]
Slots come in many kinds. Typical ones are
Attack: raises attack power
Defense: raises defense

[06614.298]
A Slot also changes the name of the equipment. {08:78 00 00 00} + Attack makes an Attack Sword, for example.

[06614.299]
Slots also have levels, and the higher the level, the greater the effect. The highest is level 8.

[06614.300]
Equipment with Slots is mostly dropped by monsters.

[06614.301]
Fight lots of monsters, collect equipment with Slots, and try making Slot equipment that is yours alone.

[06614.302]
Just between us, accessories can be refined too. But not with one another.

[06614.303]
They can only be refined as an accessory together with a piece of equipment.

[06614.304]
The advantage here is that the Slot changes with the kind of equipment you refine it with. It differs between weapons and armor.

[06614.305]
Also, since only one accessory can be equipped, putting unwanted accessories to use in refining is very effective.

[06614.306]
Besides these, outerwear, that is, "capes", can be refined with equipment just as accessories can. And in that case, believe it or not, you can add an element to the equipment.

[06614.307]
Make good use of refining with accessories, and aim to become an even finer Refine artisan.

[06614.308]

[06614.309]
Unique Items look impressive, and many have high attack power and a high Slot level, but they have only one Slot.

[06614.310]
On the other hand, Unique Items other than accessories cannot be refined. They cannot be strengthened any further.

[06614.311]
So I recommend selling off the items you no longer need.

[06614.312]
